Why Climate Action matters

SDG 13 calls on all of us to take urgent action.Here is why it matters, who it affects and what each of us can do today.

The Crisis is real

Climate change is no longer a future threat rising seas, extreme weather, and collapsing ecosystems are happening right now. Students and young people will inherit consequences of todays decision.

  • Extreme weatehr events have doubled since 2000
  • Sea levels rising 3.7mm per year and accelerating
  • 1 million species ar risk of extinction

Students can drive change

You dont need to be a politician or scientist to make a meaningful impact. As a student, you have access to communities, knowledge, and platforms that can amplify climate action in powerful ways.

  • Switch to a plant-rich diet 3+ days a week
  • Join or start a sustainabilty group on campus
  • Use public transport or cycle instead of driving

A betetr future is possible

If individuals, communities and nations act together, we can limit warming to 1.5°C and buildna fairer, cleaner world. Every actions no matter how small contributes to this shared outcome.

  • Clean energy could power 90% of the world by 2050
  • Green jobs could reach 24 million global by 2030
  • Restoring forests could absorb 10% of annual emissons
